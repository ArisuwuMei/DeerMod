## I truly love deers, that's why I decided to make this simple mod that adds those beautiful animals to Minecraft! 🦌🦌🦌

![Deer in the Plains](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/Nxu5cwsq/images/0e634a5bc67d8ab2c72bf58f19c066c5b7e0080e.webp)

### What does the mod add?
- Entity: Deer 🦌
- Items: Antelers, Venison, Cooked Venison, Deer Spawn Egg
- Shapless Recipe: Antlers -> 3 Bonemeal

### What do deers drop?
- Venison (0-3) or Cooked Venison
- Antlers (0-1)
- Antlers (1) when sheared

### What do deers eat?
- Wheat
- Sweet Berries
- Carrots

### Mechanics:
- The antlers of an adult deer grow back when it eats grass
- Baby deers don't have antlers
